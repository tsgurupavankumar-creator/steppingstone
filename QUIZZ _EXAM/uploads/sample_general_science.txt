Question 1: What is the primary gas found in the Earth's atmosphere?
A. Oxygen
B. Nitrogen
C. Carbon Dioxide
D. Hydrogen
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Nitrogen makes up approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere, while oxygen accounts for about 21%.
